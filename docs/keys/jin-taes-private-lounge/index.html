Jin-Tae's Private Lounge

Where to use the Jin-Tae's Private Lounge in MW4 DMZ: Heavenly Luck Casino penthouse, grid F4, Cheongun Village. Rarity, directions, what is inside and how to get the key.

How to get it
Carried by the Lieutenant Jin-Tae.

The grid square marks the search area, not the exact door.

Directions

Start the generator in the casino garage and take the lobby lift to the penthouse. Turn left out of the lift, cross the corridor, then turn right.

Inside

Two Legendary weapons are reported in the Lieutenant crate; also two lockers.
