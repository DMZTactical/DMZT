How to get it
Reward from the DMZ Order “Operation Grid Assault”.

The grid square marks the search area, not the exact door.

Directions

Look for a teal door on the east side of floor 14 in the Netshin Tech Complex. The route goes through the main entrance, lift shaft and balcony; expect possible robodogs and turrets.

Inside

A Lieutenant crate and two weapons lockers.
