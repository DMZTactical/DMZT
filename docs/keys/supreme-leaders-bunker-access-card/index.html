How to get it
Reward from the DMZ Order “Operation Iron Citadel”.

The grid square marks the search area, not the exact door.

Directions

Take stairwell 02 in Admin Complex No. 2 down into the bunker. Beyond the white security doors, follow the narrow passage and stairs to the large grey doors.

Inside

The Supreme Leader's rooms with a Lieutenant crate; a Legendary machine gun has been observed.
