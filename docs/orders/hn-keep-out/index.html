Keep Out

Hornet's Nest 2/2

Combine Lieutenant takedowns with the named key-room chain: obtain an eligible Lieutenant key, open its room and loot the weapon there.

Objectives

  • 3×Kill 3 Lieutenants
  • 1×Loot a Lieutenant's Key
  • 1×Enter a Lieutenant's Room and Loot the Lieutenant's Gun

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Prepare a Lieutenant hunt and verify which room a found key opens before travelling to it.

Step by step

  1. Defeat three Lieutenants and check their own takedown counter. Commanders are a separate target category.
  2. Inspect Lieutenant-related loot for a key that meets this objective. Read its name and destination rather than assuming any key will qualify.
  3. Bring the key to its matching Lieutenant room, unlock it and loot the Lieutenant weapon required by the task.

Watch out

A successful takedown should not be treated as a guaranteed key drop. Check your loot and plan another hunt if needed.
