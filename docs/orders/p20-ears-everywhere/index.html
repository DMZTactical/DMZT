Ears Everywhere

Page 20

Enter Cheonwang Peak Array, hack the indicated second-floor broadcast system and collect its Encrypted Voice Logs.

Objectives

  • 1×Enter Cheonwang Peak Array
  • 1×Hack the Communications Broadcast System on the 2nd Floor of the DBN TV Building in Cheonwang Peak Array
  • 1×Loot Encrypted Voice Logs From the Hacked Communications Broadcast System

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Where

  • E6Cheonwang Peak ArrayMedium

Items needed

  • Encrypted Voice LogsMission Items×1

Preparation

Plan the Array visit and leave backpack space for the logs.

Step by step

  1. Reach Cheonwang Peak Array and verify the location check before searching for the TV building.
  2. Inside the DBN TV building at the Array, find the second-floor Communications Broadcast System and complete its hack interaction.
  3. After the hack, search the system’s associated loot for Encrypted Voice Logs and collect the named item.

Watch out

DBN TV at Cheonwang Peak Array is not DBN Headquarters in The Grid. Follow the building named by this order.
