I'm Over Here

The Grid 1/2

Use an Inflatable Decoy to distract a drone, then track Combat Drone and Heavy Combat Drone eliminations separately.

Objectives

  • 1×Distract a Drone With an Inflatable Decoy
  • 8×Destroy 8 Combat Drones
  • 3×Destroy 3 Heavy Combat Drones

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Items needed

  • Inflatable DecoyField Upgrades×1

Preparation

Pack an Inflatable Decoy and prepare ammunition and armor before fighting multiple drones.

Step by step

  1. Deploy the Inflatable Decoy where it can attract a drone and verify the distraction check before moving on.
  2. Destroy eight qualifying Combat Drones. Read the target type and its own counter rather than adding every drone to this total.
  3. Destroy three Heavy Combat Drones and confirm that the heavy-target counter advances.

Watch out

An Inflatable Decoy and a Decoy Grenade are different items. Bring the one named by this order.
