Information Power

Page 11

Purchase Lieutenant intel at the FOB Boss Board, then make a Lieutenant takedown. Plan the hunt before committing to the fight.

Objectives

  • 1×Pay for Lieutenant Intel on the Boss Board
  • 1×Kill a Lieutenant

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Check your available intel choices and bring a combat-ready loadout suited to the selected area.

Step by step

  1. At the Boss Board, pay for Lieutenant intel and verify that the purchase objective registers.
  2. Follow the hunt information, identify the Lieutenant and complete the takedown. Check the Lieutenant counter rather than counting any powerful enemy.

Watch out

Lieutenants and Commanders are separate target groups in this app and in the listed objectives.
