The Vault

Page 20

Restore access to a Hanex branch vault in Cheongun Village or Wolves Stadium. Power, access card and keypad steps are distinct parts of entering it.

Objectives

  • 1×Enter a Hanex Branch Bank in Cheongun Village or Wolves Stadium
  • 1×Reboot Power to Access Control Systems
  • 1×Find a Way Inside the Vault

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Recommended: bring a Car Battery and plan space to carry the Bank Branch Vault Access card found in the bank.

Step by step

  1. Enter one of the two branch locations named by this objective. Do not substitute the main Hanex tower simply because it shares the bank name.
  2. Use a Car Battery at the bank’s power panel to restore the access system. Follow the actual interaction prompt.
  3. Find the branch access card, use the vault reader and read the bank’s code clue. The clue supplies three digits; work out the remaining digit at the keypad.

Watch out

There is no universal bank code to enter from a guide. Use the clue and access sequence in your current match.
