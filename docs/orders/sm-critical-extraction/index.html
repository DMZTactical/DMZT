Operation Critical Extraction

Story mission

Escort General Kang through Mirae General Hospital to the evacuation helicopter. The hospital route is an escort sequence, so stay with the patient rather than racing ahead.

Objectives

  • 1×Approach the hospital
  • 1×Find General Kang
  • 1×Move Kang to safety
  • 1×Wait for the elevators
  • 1×Secure Kang in the exfil helicopter

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Where

  • F5Operation Critical ExtractionHospital: find General Kang and extract him alive.

Preparation

Recommended: armor, healing and ammunition. If playing in a squad, agree who pushes the bed and who covers the route.

Step by step

  1. Follow the mission marker to Mirae General Hospital. Clear the immediate approach before entering.
  2. Follow the interior marker through the wards to Kang’s bed. Check that the escort phase starts before leaving the room.
  3. Stand behind the bed and push it along the indicated corridor route. Cover doorway threats while moving the patient.
  4. Bring the bed to the elevators and hold the area until the elevator progress finishes. Do not leave the patient for unrelated loot.
  5. Continue to the roof and push the bed into the evacuation helicopter via its ramp. Wait for mission completion before marking the final check.

Watch out

Getting your own Operator to the roof is not the same as securing Kang in the helicopter.
