Jail Break

Hornet's Nest 2/2

Fight in the prison and Citadel areas and destroy a reinforcement helicopter. Keep the two location-dependent kill counts distinct.

Objectives

  • 15×Kill 15 Enemies in 14th Political Prison
  • 1×Destroy a Reinforcement Helicopter
  • 10×Kill 10 Enemies in the Citadel

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Where

  • C314th Political PrisonHigh

Preparation

Prepare for a fortified area and choose equipment capable of engaging the helicopter as well as infantry.

Step by step

  1. Make 15 qualifying kills within 14th Political Prison and monitor the area-specific counter.
  2. Identify and destroy a reinforcement helicopter, then check that this aircraft objective registered.
  3. Move into The Citadel and make ten qualifying kills there. Confirm the smaller location’s check instead of adding all prison kills to it.

Watch out

The helicopter requirement is not simply any airborne target, and the prison and Citadel checks are not interchangeable.
