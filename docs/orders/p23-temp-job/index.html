Temp Job

Page 23

Accumulate completed Side Ops while following purposeful raid routes. This objective counts the activity completions, not simply time spent in Hajin.

Objectives

  • 40×Complete 40 Side Ops

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Include optional activities close to your planned stops instead of making long detours for each one.

Step by step

  1. Finish Side Ops until the qualifying total reaches 40. Check the activity-complete message and the order count before adding another manual completion.

Watch out

Side Ops and Dynamic Ops are named separately. Use the game’s classification rather than treating every mission event as a Side Op.
