Blindness

Human Intel 2/2

Prepare Flash Grenades, then make eliminations while enemies are affected by them. Printing and combat have different counters.

Objectives

  • 4×Print 4 Flash Grenades
  • 8×Kill 8 Flashed Enemies

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Items needed

  • SyntheticsResources×40
  • ChemicalsResources×40

Preparation

Check the Flash recipe and materials in the printer. Plan room for the grenades in your loadout.

Step by step

  1. Print four Flash Grenades and confirm that the crafting counter advances. Merely owning four grenades is not the listed crafting action.
  2. Flash a manageable group, then eliminate affected enemies promptly. Observe the flashed-enemy counter rather than assuming every nearby kill qualified.

Watch out

Throwing a Flash is not itself a kill. Keep the effect and the elimination connected in each attempt.
