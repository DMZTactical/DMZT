Radiation Station

Page 7

Visit a Fire Station, obtain the specified protective loot and search containers while in radiation. Prepare protection before doing the exposure-dependent step.

Objectives

  • 1×Locate a Fire Station
  • 3×Loot 3 Gas Masks or Radiation Pill Bottles
  • 5×Search 5 Containers While in Radiation

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Recommended: gas protection, healing and a planned route back out of the affected area.

Step by step

  1. Locate a Fire Station using its map category and enter the location to register the visit.
  2. Loot the Gas Masks or Radiation Pill Bottles named by the objective until its count reaches three. Check the current game wording for eligible items.
  3. While in an affected radiation area, search five containers and monitor protection throughout. Check the task count rather than assuming containers outside the zone qualify.

Watch out

Possessing a mask does not mean it will remain effective indefinitely. Leave or replenish protection before it is exhausted.
