Crashed Crate

Page 10

Combine Lost Supply Drop searches with a Loot Radio Tower Side Op. These are named activities, not simply three ordinary crates and any tower.

Objectives

  • 3×Retrieve 3 Lost Supply Drops
  • 1×Complete a Loot Radio Tower Side Op

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Choose a route through possible supply-drop areas and leave time for a tower climb.

Step by step

  1. Find and retrieve three qualifying Lost Supply Drops. Confirm each pickup through its activity or order counter rather than relying only on a static map marker.
  2. Start a Loot Radio Tower Side Op and complete its indicated tower-loot action. A Lieutenant Hunt terminal is a different activity.

Watch out

A possible drop point does not confirm an active drop in this match. Follow the current game prompts.

Page rewards

  • Large Backpack
  • 20,000 DMZ Cash
  • Basic Triage Kit
