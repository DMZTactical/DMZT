Civil Service

The Grid 2/2

Travel through NuriGO Mall to City Hall, collect the Financial Ledger Fragments and extract with them. The final item must leave the map with you.

Objectives

  • 1×Enter NuriGO Mall
  • 1×Locate City Hall
  • 1×Locate and Extract the Financial Ledger Fragments on the Second Floor of City Hall

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Where

  • H6NuriGo MallHigh

Preparation

Reserve inventory space and plan an extraction route before searching City Hall.

Step by step

  1. Enter NuriGO Mall and check the location objective before continuing toward City Hall.
  2. Find City Hall using the current objective information. Do not confuse the named building with the entire mall area.
  3. Search City Hall’s second floor for Financial Ledger Fragments, take the named item and extract successfully while retaining it.

Watch out

Picking up the fragments completes only part of the final action. Do not sell, discard or leave them behind before extraction.
