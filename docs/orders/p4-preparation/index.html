Preparation

Page 4

Use the FOB printer to make the two required equipment pieces. This is a crafting task, not a shopping or looting requirement.

Objectives

  • 1×Print a Medium Backpack
  • 1×Print a 2-Plate Armor Vest

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Items needed

  • SyntheticsResources×100
  • Rigid SyntheticsIngredients×5
  • Reactive MetalsIngredients×5

Preparation

Open each recipe first and check its material requirements before spending resources elsewhere.

Step by step

  1. Select the Medium Backpack recipe and print the backpack. Check that you chose the medium version rather than another size.
  2. Print the 2-Plate Armor Vest recipe and verify the crafting check in game. A looted vest is not the action specified by this objective.

Watch out

If printing is unavailable, inspect the recipe’s lock and material requirements in your current game rather than guessing what is missing.
