Watch for Bumps

Underground 1/2

Acquire an enemy vehicle, service it at a Gas Station and use a vehicle for seven enemy run-overs. Keep these actions separate in your checklist.

Objectives

  • 1×Steal an Enemy Vehicle
  • 1×Refuel and Repair a Vehicle at a Gas Station
  • 7×Run Over 7 Enemies in a Vehicle

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Identify a vehicle route with a Gas Station and room to turn around. Avoid committing to a damaged vehicle without an exit plan.

Step by step

  1. Take an enemy vehicle and check that the theft objective registers before travelling far.
  2. Bring the vehicle to a Gas Station and complete both refuelling and repair. Check its state and the task indicator before leaving.
  3. Run over enemies with a vehicle until the qualifying count reaches seven. Pick approaches with space to manoeuvre rather than getting trapped among enemies.

Watch out

A weapon kill while sitting in a vehicle is not automatically a run-over kill.
