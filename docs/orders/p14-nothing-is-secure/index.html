Nothing Is Secure

Page 14

Open four safes and extract with the required combined inventory value. Treat safe openings and extraction value as independent counters.

Objectives

  • 4×Open 4 Safes
  • 20000×Extract $20000 Total Backpack and Loadout Value

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Preparation

Choose a safe route and check backpack space before taking valuable loot.

Step by step

  1. Complete four safe openings and verify each one in the safe counter. Merely finding a locked safe is not an opening.
  2. Before exiting, check that backpack and loadout value together meet 20,000 DMZ Cash. Extract with that value and confirm credit.

Watch out

Bank balance and extracted inventory value are different measures. Use the value shown for the objective, not your total savings.
