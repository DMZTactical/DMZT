Abandoned

Page 18

Enter NuriGO Mall’s underground area, destroy K9s and extract intact Pristine K9 Battery Cores. The quality of the cores matters.

Objectives

  • 1×Enter the Underground in NuriGO Mall
  • 10×Kill 10 K9s
  • 3×Extract 3 Pristine K9 Battery Cores

Objectives in game are authoritative.

Where

  • H6NuriGo MallHigh

Preparation

Leave three inventory slots or suitable stack space and prepare for close encounters with K9s.

Step by step

  1. Reach the underground beneath NuriGO Mall and confirm the location check. Surface mall loot is not the named area.
  2. Destroy ten K9s, using the K9 counter to confirm eligible targets.
  3. Collect intact cores promptly before a downed K9’s explosion can destroy them, then extract three Pristine K9 Battery Cores.

Watch out

Damaged cores or cores left behind do not meet an intact-core extraction requirement. Read the item name and keep it until exfil.

Page rewards

  • Nitro Compounds x2
  • SERE Kit
  • 20,000 DMZ Cash
